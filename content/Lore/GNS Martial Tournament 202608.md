### Analysis
#### Beef
* Speaking with [[hekapider|heka]] as advice for tanks 
* Here is what i would say, without good practice and experience, your chance of beating gns is very lopsided, in a best of multiple match situation. Tank are too ping dependent and doesn't do enough dps in pvp if you don't have ping combos. Gns knows how to fight tanks and he prefer slow and drawn out matches to surgically strike. Your best bet at winning without a lot of experience is to pick a high burst dmg class and just focus on fast dmg and attacks to force mistakes from gns when he has to react to fast attacks and don't pause. Dont give him a chance to think/react. When I legit try to fight gns it is very sweaty.

#### soul
- I will say don't fight Gns with ropebrella lol he stays perfectly just out of reach all the time.  
- He loves to dash/dodge backwards too from combos and talon strikes. 
- He's always trying to meridian into the guardian palm so either block or parry if you see him disappear suddenly

#### gns
##### General
* Like all mythic players I will first fish for your serene breeze usage (rex calls this the dance)
* I am parry happy but not spamming aka I like to try and parry exactly what is needed because parry spamming in soulslike games equals death
	* this does not apply to wwm though just a force of habit from me
	* if i am on my game I can parry most attacks except the rat & strat sword and can parry most parry-able mystics 
		* the best example is when fighting mei ... because of lag due to distance, I am listening for mei's moves rather that watching and this is how I parry hengdao special and if I am doing this, this is a good indication I am in the zone
		* also I parry on condition with those that spar a lot with me.  Everyone has tendencies (myself included as they are being listed here, lol) and I will parry/read your move before you do it.
			* all of the mythic players have this skill as we spar a lot together and we play a lot of arena 1s
* Tenacity into and attacking player is my second defensive option.  Apply defense with offense, lol
* Dodging is my third option especially as I am using the "noodle build" aka gauntlets.   I am looking to perfect dodge into divine counter
* Block counter is my least use defensive technique (aka I am a bad tank player). Also, if i am doing this without success this will indicate I am being backed into a corner as per beef's analysis.  The flip side is if I am landing the divine counter it means I am doing it on purpose and reading your move.
