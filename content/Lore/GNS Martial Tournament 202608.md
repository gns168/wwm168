### Analysis
#### Beef
* Speaking with [[hekapider|heka]] as advice for tanks 
* Here is what i would say, without good practice and experience, your chance of beating gns is very lopsided, in a best of multiple match situation. Tank are too ping dependent and doesn't do enough dps in pvp if you don't have ping combos. Gns knows how to fight tanks and he prefer slow and drawn out matches to surgically strike. Your best bet at winning without a lot of experience is to pick a high burst dmg class and just focus on fast dmg and attacks to force mistakes from gns when he has to react to fast attacks and don't pause. Dont give him a chance to think/react. When I legit try to fight gns it is very sweaty.

#### gns
##### General
* Like all mythic players I will first fish for your serene breeze usage (rex calls this the dance)
* I am parry happy but not spamming aka I like to parry exactly what is needed because parry spamming in soulslike equals death
	* this does not apply to wwm though just a force of habit from me
	* if i am on my game I can parry most attacks except the rat & strat sword and can parry most mystics 
* Tenacity into and attacking player is my second defensive option.  Apply defense with offense, lol
* Dodging is my third option especially as I am using the "noodle build" aka gauntlets because I am looking to perfect parry into divine counter
* Block counter is my least use defensive technique (aka I am a bad tank player)

##### Fanbrella
This is my worse matchup.  Because I am parry happy and will parry the stun out of reflex.  I also play riskier because I want to style on Rex (aka all fanbrella as a result) and I will time your stun by trying to iframe through it. But if I miss time it against cai or Rex it's game over for me.

I haven't played fanbrella since season 1 of arena... so I may not remember the combos and have the muscle memory to execute them even if I did.  But the longer the match runs the worse it will be especially if it comes back.  No I don't intend to practice it.

##### Tank, Healer
If I really wanted to win aka sweat then I will kite you.  As per what beef mention, I will slow play this match looking for opportunity to surgically strike and combo you.  Then I run.  But since this is guild friendly I dont do this.  I will purposely attack you to bait dodge counter then I will avoid the first two divine counters and run into to parry the third.  I can also reliable parry the vortex (been telling beef to talon out of the vortex to stop this maybe he's conditioning me, shrugs)

Unless you have enough dps to kill this build you will not be able to win

##### Hengdao
This one is going to be tough because it is one of my strongest builds and I can mindless get to mythic quite easily.  Because it used to be meta it can reliably parry most of the attacks unless I am having an offday

##### Dustbrella
This one will also be tough because it was a struggle for me to get to mythic the first two tries at mythic so I had to play ALOOOT of matches ie have the most experience in arena with it.  This season I am clipping at about 85% win rate with long winning streaks ... as high as 20.  I also enjoy playing this over the hengdao (which I play mindlessly/carelessly.

I can reliably parry the umbrella but will keep away from the rope.

AS for playing against it, I play keep away and force you to come in.  I will toss the umbrella to see if you will parry spam.  IF you do I will teleport and talon strike you.  Once you are on the ground you will be in trouble aka "rex dirty tricks" 

##### Nameless
Unless you have t6 nameless and know how to use the constant sword waves. I will weave in and out to avoid the spear stun and will be counter attacking it.  I should practice dodge the sword waves but I tend to try and parry it; both are luke warm in terms of success
##### Rats and Strat sword
I will typically drunken poet into combo I dont like playing against either so rex, soul and beef are thinking along the same lines.
