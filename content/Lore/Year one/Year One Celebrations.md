# Contest & Rules

## Award Season 
Sponsored by [[avenuex 顾明|Leader]]
To celebrated the anniversary of this Guild, AvenueX will be doing an "Award Season" in Early December. Here are the perks:
    
1. The following members will each get a Battle Pass for their continuous participation and contribution to our guild and this server: [[gns]], [[soulren|Soul]], [[lonerexlapis|LoneRexLapis]], [[weiyun 组玮芸]], [[dongfunbubai|DongFanBuBai]], [[hekapider|heka]]
	 
2. 1 Battle Pass will go to the Most Active Member accumulatively over the past year based on the points on the member roaster, if it happens to overlap with the 6 members above mentioned, then it will go to the next most active member.
	    
3. 1 skin of choice (default 2580 but if you really want a 1280 that is also fine, totally depending on personal preference) purchasable by pearls to a random winner among the first 40 most active members accumulatively over the past year, you also need to be active in the last month (30 days) to be qualified for the draw.
	    
4. 3 Monthly Passes randomly drawn among all members who are active within the last month (30 days)
	    
All draws will exclude alt characters and guild leader. List will be finalized on December 5th Saturday, and winners drawn on December 6th Sunday.
